Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-10-22 · Operator: TianHai

Welcome to Burger Boss (the "App"), operated by TianHai ("we", "us"). This Privacy Policy explains how information related to the App is collected, used, stored, and shared. By using the App, you acknowledge this Policy.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Game Progress and Local Data

The App primarily stores progress, settings, shop name, and in-app entitlement status on your device. We generally do not upload this data to our own servers. Uninstalling the App or clearing app data may erase local progress.

1.2 In-App Purchases (IAP)

Purchases (such as diamonds, growth packs, or ad removal) are processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play. We may receive purchase success status, product identifiers, and receipt/transaction information needed to grant or restore items. We do not receive your full payment card number or full payment account password.

1.3 Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App uses Google AdMob for rewarded video ads. Ad services may collect or process device identifiers, IP address, device/app information, and ad interaction data to deliver ads, measure performance, and (where allowed) personalize ads. See the Google Privacy Policy and AdMob Help.

1.4 System Sharing

If you use the system share sheet, content is handled by the third-party apps you choose. We do not collect your contacts or social-account passwords through this feature.

1.5 Diagnostics (if enabled)

Platforms or SDKs may collect technical data such as device model, OS version, and crash logs to improve stability.

2. How We Use Information

  • Provide, maintain, and improve the App;
  • Process and verify purchases and restore entitlements;
  • Show ads, measure ad performance, and manage consent where required;
  • Protect against fraud and abuse;
  • Comply with law and store policies.

3. Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with:

  • Service providers, such as Google (AdMob) and Apple/Google (stores and payments);
  • Legal authorities, when required by law or to protect rights;
  • Business transfers, such as merger or acquisition, subject to this Policy.

4. Ad Consent and Tracking

In the EEA/UK and other applicable regions, we use Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) for ad-related consent. Where shown, you can open Privacy Options in the App settings. On iOS, the system may show an App Tracking Transparency prompt; you may decline.

5. Children's Privacy

The App is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13 (or a higher age required by local law). We do not knowingly collect children's personal information. Contact us if you believe such data was collected in error.

6. Retention and Security

Local game data remains on your device until you delete the App or clear its data. Payment and advertising data are retained by the relevant platforms under their policies. We use reasonable safeguards, but no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

7. Your Rights

Depending on applicable law (e.g., GDPR, CCPA), you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, object to personalized ads, withdraw consent, or request portability. You can manage advertising identifiers in device settings, or contact us below. Purchase and refund requests should generally go through the App Store or Google Play.

8. International Transfers

Service providers may process data outside your country. We expect appropriate safeguards consistent with applicable law.

9. Changes

We may update this Policy and will revise the effective date on this page. For material changes, we may provide notice in the App or by other reasonable means.
